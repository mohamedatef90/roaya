## 1. Brand Identity

### 1.1 Brand Overview

**Roaya IT** (رؤيا لتقنية المعلومات) is an Egyptian IT services company that combines local expertise with global infrastructure through its partnership with WorldPosta.

**Brand Positioning:**
- **Primary:** Trusted IT Partner for Egyptian Businesses
- **Secondary:** Transparent, Local, Results-Driven
- **Tertiary:** Enterprise-Grade Technology with Egyptian Support

### 1.2 Brand Values

Our brand is built on six core values that should be reflected in all design work:

1. **Transparency** - Open, honest, clear communication
2. **Egyptian First** - Local expertise, cultural understanding
3. **Results Over Features** - Business outcomes, not just technology
4. **Partnership** - Long-term relationships, not transactions
5. **Excellence** - Global standards, quality delivery
6. **Integrity** - Do what's right, always

### 1.3 Brand Personality

**Professional** - Enterprise-grade, trustworthy, reliable  
**Innovative** - Modern technology, cutting-edge solutions  
**Approachable** - Friendly, local, understanding  
**Transparent** - Clear, honest, straightforward  
**Egyptian** - Proudly local, culturally aware

### 1.4 Target Audience

**Primary:**
- Egyptian IT Directors and CTOs
- Egyptian CEOs and Business Owners
- SMBs seeking enterprise-grade IT services

**Secondary:**
- International companies with Egyptian operations
- Government entities requiring IT services
- Educational institutions

### 1.5 Brand Messaging Pillars

1. **Transparency First** - "See pricing before you commit"
2. **Local + Global** - "Egyptian expertise, global infrastructure"
3. **Guaranteed ROI** - "Measurable business outcomes"
4. **24/7 Support** - "Always here when you need us"
5. **WorldPosta Partnership** - "Enterprise-grade technology"

## 2. Logo Usage

### 2.1 Logo Files

**Primary Logo Files:**
- `roaya-logo.png` - Full color logo (PNG)
- `roaya-logo.webp` - Full color logo (WebP, optimized)
- `roaya-mini.png` - Compact/mini version

**Location:** `/roaya-website/src/assets/images/`

### 2.2 Logo Variations

#### Full Logo (Horizontal)
**Usage:** Website headers, business cards, presentations, email signatures

**Specifications:**
- Minimum width: 200px
- Clear space: 50% of logo height on all sides
- Background: White or light backgrounds (use full color)
- Dark backgrounds: Use white/light version if available

#### Mini/Compact Logo
**Usage:** Social media profile pictures, favicons, small spaces

**Specifications:**
- Minimum size: 40px × 40px
- Maintain aspect ratio
- Clear space: 10px minimum on all sides

### 2.3 Logo Placement

**Do's:**
- ✅ Place logo in top-left (LTR) or top-right (RTL) corners
- ✅ Maintain clear space around logo
- ✅ Use appropriate size for context
- ✅ Ensure high contrast with background

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never stretch or distort logo
- ❌ Never rotate logo
- ❌ Never place on busy backgrounds without clear space
- ❌ Never use logo smaller than minimum size
- ❌ Never alter colors (except approved variations)

### 2.4 Logo on Backgrounds

**Light Backgrounds:**
- Use full-color logo
- Ensure sufficient contrast (WCAG AA minimum)

**Dark Backgrounds:**
- Use white/light version if available
- If not available, use full-color with sufficient contrast
- Test contrast ratio: minimum 4.5:1

**Gradient Backgrounds:**
- Use white/light version for best visibility
- Ensure logo stands out clearly
- Consider adding subtle shadow or outline if needed

**Photography Backgrounds:**
- Use white/light version with drop shadow
- Place in corner with sufficient clear space
- Ensure logo is clearly visible

### 2.5 Logo in Social Media

**Profile Pictures:**
- Use mini/compact logo
- Square format: 400px × 400px minimum
- Center logo with clear space
- Background: White or brand color

**Cover Images:**
- Include full logo in top-left or top-right
- Minimum logo width: 150px
- Maintain clear space
- Ensure logo doesn't compete with cover image

**Post Graphics:**
- Logo in bottom-right corner (small)
- Minimum width: 80px
- Opacity: 80-90% for subtle branding
- Never place logo in center (reserved for content)

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Font Families

#### English: Inter
**Google Fonts:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@300;400;500;600;700;800;900&display=swap`

**Weights Available:**
- 300: Light
- 400: Regular (Normal)
- 500: Medium
- 600: Semibold
- 700: Bold
- 800: Extrabold
- 900: Black

**Usage:**
- All English text
- Headlines, body text, UI elements
- Marketing materials
- Social media graphics
- Video titles and captions

#### Arabic: Tajawal
**Google Fonts:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Tajawal:wght@300;400;500;700;800;900&display=swap`

**Weights Available:**
- 300: Light
- 400: Regular (Normal)
- 500: Medium
- 700: Bold
- 800: Extrabold
- 900: Black

**Usage:**
- All Arabic text
- RTL layouts
- Bilingual materials
- Arabic social media content

### 4.2 Typography Scale

#### Headings

**H1 - Hero Titles**
- Size: `clamp(2.25rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)` (36px - 72px)
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Letter Spacing: -0.02em
- Usage: Main headlines, hero sections, landing pages

**H2 - Section Headers**
- Size: `clamp(1.875rem, 4vw, 3.75rem)` (30px - 60px)
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Letter Spacing: -0.01em
- Usage: Section titles, page headers

**H3 - Subsection Headers**
- Size: `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 3rem)` (24px - 48px)
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Usage: Subsection titles, card headers

**H4 - Card Titles**
- Size: `clamp(1.25rem, 2.5vw, 2.25rem)` (20px - 36px)
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Usage: Card titles, feature names

**H5 - Small Headings**
- Size: `clamp(1.125rem, 2vw, 1.5rem)` (18px - 24px)
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Usage: Small section headers, labels

**H6 - Minor Headings**
- Size: `clamp(1rem, 1.5vw, 1.25rem)` (16px - 20px)
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Usage: Minor headers, captions

#### Body Text

**Lead Text (Hero Descriptions)**
- Size: `clamp(1.125rem, 2vw, 1.5rem)` (18px - 24px)
- Weight: 400 (Regular)
- Line Height: 1.6
- Usage: Hero descriptions, important paragraphs

**Body Large**
- Size: 18px (1.125rem)
- Weight: 400 (Regular)
- Line Height: 1.75
- Usage: Large body text, featured content

**Body Regular**
- Size: 16px (1rem)
- Weight: 400 (Regular)
- Line Height: 1.5
- Usage: Standard body text, paragraphs

**Body Small**
- Size: 14px (0.875rem)
- Weight: 400 (Regular)
- Line Height: 1.5
- Usage: Small text, captions, metadata

**Caption**
- Size: 12px (0.75rem)
- Weight: 400 (Regular)
- Line Height: 1.5
- Usage: Captions, footnotes, fine print

### 4.3 Typography Usage Guidelines

**Marketing Materials:**
- Use Inter for all English text
- Use Tajawal for all Arabic text
- Maintain hierarchy: H1 > H2 > H3 > Body
- Use bold (700) for emphasis, not regular text
- Maintain line height for readability

**Social Media:**
- Headlines: Bold (700), 24-36px
- Body: Regular (400), 16-18px
- CTAs: Semibold (600), 16-20px
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use high contrast (4.5:1 minimum)

**Videos:**
- Titles: Bold (700), 32-48px
- Subtitles: Semibold (600), 20-24px
- Body: Regular (400), 18-20px
- Ensure text is readable on video backgrounds
- Use text shadows or outlines if needed

**Print Materials:**
- Use same font families as digital
- Minimum body text: 10pt
- Headlines: 18-24pt minimum
- Consider print resolution (300 DPI minimum)

## 5. Imagery & Photography

### 5.1 Photography Style

**Brand Photography Characteristics:**
- Professional, clean, modern
- Natural lighting preferred
- Egyptian business context
- Diverse, inclusive representation
- Technology-focused but human-centered
- Bright, optimistic tone

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use high-quality, professional photography
- ✅ Show Egyptian business environments
- ✅ Include diverse people
- ✅ Use natural, bright lighting
- ✅ Focus on technology and people together
- ✅ Maintain consistent color grading

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Avoid stock photos that look generic
- ❌ Don't use overly staged or fake-looking images
- ❌ Avoid dark, moody photography
- ❌ Don't use images that don't reflect Egyptian context
- ❌ Avoid low-resolution images
- ❌ Don't use images with poor composition

### 5.2 Image Treatment

**Color Grading:**
- Slight desaturation (5-10%)
- Warm tones preferred
- Maintain brand color harmony
- Consistent look across all images

**Overlays:**
- Use brand gradients for overlays
- Opacity: 20-40% for subtle effects
- Use overlays to improve text readability
- Maintain image visibility

**Filters:**
- Minimal filters preferred
- Subtle contrast adjustments
- Slight sharpening for clarity
- Avoid heavy filters or effects

### 5.3 Image Sizes & Formats

**Website:**
- Hero images: 1920px × 1080px (16:9)
- Card images: 800px × 600px (4:3)
- Thumbnails: 400px × 300px (4:3)
- Format: WebP (preferred) or PNG/JPEG
- Compression: Optimized for web

**Social Media:**
- Facebook cover: 1200px × 630px
- Facebook post: 1200px × 1200px (square)
- Instagram post: 1080px × 1080px (square)
- Instagram story: 1080px × 1920px (9:16)
- LinkedIn cover: 1128px × 191px
- Twitter header: 1500px × 500px
- Format: JPEG or PNG
- File size: < 5MB

**Print:**
- Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Format: TIFF or high-quality JPEG
- Color space: CMYK for print
- Consider bleed areas (3mm minimum)

**Videos:**
- Thumbnails: 1280px × 720px (16:9)
- Format: JPEG or PNG
- High quality, clear imagery

### 5.4 Iconography

**Icon Style:**
- Modern, minimalist
- Consistent stroke width (2px)
- Rounded corners preferred
- Clear, recognizable at small sizes

**Icon Library:**
- Primary: Lucide Icons (via ng-icons)
- Style: Outline icons preferred
- Color: Use brand colors
- Size: Consistent sizing (16px, 24px, 32px, 48px)

**Usage:**
- Feature icons: 48px (3rem)
- UI icons: 24px (1.5rem)
- Small icons: 16px (1rem)
- Social media: 24-32px

## 10. Do's and Don'ts

### 10.1 Logo Usage

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use logo at appropriate size (minimum 200px width for full logo)
- ✅ Maintain clear space around logo (50% of logo height)
- ✅ Use full-color logo on light backgrounds
- ✅ Use white/light version on dark backgrounds
- ✅ Maintain aspect ratio
- ✅ Place logo in prominent but not dominant position

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never stretch or distort logo
- ❌ Never rotate logo
- ❌ Never alter logo colors (except approved variations)
- ❌ Never place logo on busy backgrounds without clear space
- ❌ Never use logo smaller than minimum size
- ❌ Never add effects (shadows, outlines) unless approved

### 10.2 Color Usage

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use Primary Navy as primary brand color
- ✅ Use Secondary Teal for accents and CTAs
- ✅ Use Accent Purple sparingly for premium content
- ✅ Maintain 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum (WCAG AA)
- ✅ Use gradients for backgrounds and CTAs
- ✅ Test colors in both light and dark modes

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never use colors outside brand palette without approval
- ❌ Never use low contrast color combinations
- ❌ Never use brand colors in ways that conflict with brand identity
- ❌ Never use more than 3 brand colors in single design
- ❌ Never use colors that don't meet accessibility standards

### 10.3 Typography

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use Inter for all English text
- ✅ Use Tajawal for all Arabic text
- ✅ Maintain typography hierarchy
- ✅ Use appropriate font weights
- ✅ Ensure readable font sizes (minimum 12px for body)
- ✅ Maintain proper line height for readability

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never use fonts outside brand font families
- ❌ Never use more than 3 font sizes in single design
- ❌ Never use decorative or script fonts
- ❌ Never use all caps for body text
- ❌ Never use font sizes smaller than 12px for body text
- ❌ Never use low contrast text

### 10.4 Imagery

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use high-quality, professional photography
- ✅ Use images that reflect Egyptian business context
- ✅ Maintain consistent color grading
- ✅ Use appropriate image sizes for context
- ✅ Optimize images for web (compression, format)
- ✅ Include alt text for accessibility

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never use low-resolution images
- ❌ Never use generic stock photos that don't reflect brand
- ❌ Never use images with poor composition
- ❌ Never use images that conflict with brand values
- ❌ Never use copyrighted images without permission
- ❌ Never use images that don't represent target audience

### 10.5 Social Media

**Do's:**
- ✅ Maintain consistent visual style across platforms
- ✅ Use brand colors consistently
- ✅ Include logo in appropriate places
- ✅ Keep text concise and scannable
- ✅ Use high-quality images
- ✅ Include clear CTAs

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never use inconsistent branding across platforms
- ❌ Never use low-quality images
- ❌ Never overcrowd designs with too much text
- ❌ Never use colors outside brand palette
- ❌ Never forget to include logo
- ❌ Never use unprofessional language or imagery

### 10.6 Videos

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use brand colors for graphics and overlays
- ✅ Include logo in intro/outro
- ✅ Maintain consistent color grading
- ✅ Use brand typography
- ✅ Include captions/subtitles
- ✅ Ensure high production quality

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Never use colors outside brand palette
- ❌ Never forget to include branding
- ❌ Never use inconsistent styling
- ❌ Never use low-quality video
- ❌ Never forget accessibility (captions)
- ❌ Never use unprofessional content
