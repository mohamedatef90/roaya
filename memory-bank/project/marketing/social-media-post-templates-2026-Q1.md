```

If you handle customer data - and almost every business does -

PDPL requires and how to prepare."

[SECTION 1: What is PDPL - 0:40-1:30]
"PDPL stands for Personal Data Protection Law. It came into effect
in 2020 but enforcement begins November 2026.

The law protects personal data - things like:
- Names and addresses
- Phone numbers and emails
- Financial information
- Health records

Basically, any information that can identify a person."

[SECTION 2: Who it affects - 1:30-2:30]
"If you think 'this doesn't apply to me' - think again.

PDPL applies to:
- Any business with customer databases
- HR departments with employee records
- Marketing teams with email lists
- Healthcare providers with patient data
- Banks and financial institutions

If you collect ANY personal data, you're covered."

[SECTION 3: Key Requirements - 2:30-4:00]
"Here are the main things PDPL requires:

1. Consent - Get clear permission before collecting data
2. Purpose - Only use data for what you said you would
3. Security - Protect data from breaches
4. Rights - Let people access, correct, or delete their data
5. Notification - Report breaches within 72 hours

Penalties for non-compliance include:
- Fines up to EGP 5 million
- Criminal liability for serious violations
- Reputation damage"

[SECTION 4: How to Prepare - 4:00-4:40]
"Here's your action plan:

1. Audit your data - Know what you collect and where it's stored
2. Update your policies - Privacy policy, consent forms
3. Implement security - Encryption, access controls
4. Train your team - Everyone needs to understand their role
5. Get help - Consider a compliance assessment"

[CTA - 4:40-5:00]
"We've created a free PDPL Readiness Checklist - link in the
description. And if you need help with compliance, Roaya IT
specializes in data protection.

is your company ready for PDPL?"
```
